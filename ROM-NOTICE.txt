A4ROM.DMP is firmware supplied by the owner of the original Eureka A4 dump.
It is data consumed by the emulator and is not covered by LICENSE.txt.
Do not redistribute the ROM unless you have the necessary rights.

Embedding the dump in the executable does not change this. Compiling the
firmware into the binary is still distribution of the firmware, only less
visible. If the emulator is ever published, the ROM must stay a separate
file that the user supplies from their own machine.

The same applies to the Eureka A4 Technical Manual and its development disk
(Robotron, and Turbo Pascal from Borland). That material was in eurekatech/
until 20 September 2026 and now lives outside the tree, exactly like the
ROM: set EUREKATECH to point at it. Its own PUVOD.md, which lists who holds
what, moved with it.

Two things need it and both say so when it is missing rather than passing
quietly: tools/check_io_names.py exits 2, and run-tests.bat skips the `com`
and `wp` modes -- both run READ.COM from the development disk -- leaving
fifteen tests instead of seventeen. Everything else builds and runs without
it: the findings taken from the manual are written up in our own words in
hardware-map.md, HANDOFF.md and src/eureka_io.h.
